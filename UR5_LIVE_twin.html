Live twin — the real VL-JEPA encoder and the cognitive twin answering in real time

This page has no precomputed data. Every frame of the video on the left is captured (RGB, downscaled ≤ 480 px), posted to python live_twin.py --serve, turned by the server into the 48×64 letterboxed gray input through the same ffmpeg filter chain the model was trained with (shown below the video exactly as fed to the encoder), encoded by the checkpointed VL-JEPA encoder into a 128-d latent (printed verbatim on the right), and interpreted by the next-stage twin (plan, expectations, grounding, validation gate). Latencies are the server's own measurements. Switch to your webcam or open any video file: the models will answer — and, on a scene they have never seen, they should say so.
