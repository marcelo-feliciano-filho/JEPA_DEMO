Two robots and a human in one video — per-agent cognitive twins on a shared VL-JEPA

Simulated work cell rendered in Python (sim3d.py): robot A and robot B do pick-and-place, a human walks in, takes a part from the shelf, hands it over in front of robot B and leaves; robot B must hold while the human is inside its zone. ONE encoder pass per window; each agent's region selects its tubelet tokens (region = simulator box; a tracker in a real deployment) → one vector per agent; the scene vector is context. Each agent is a stream of the fleet engine with its type's grammar.
