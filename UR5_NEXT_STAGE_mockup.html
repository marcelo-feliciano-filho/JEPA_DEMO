Cognitive Digital Twin — next-stage prediction (Stage-JEPA mock-up)

Perception is VL-JEPA (every window → a latent on the shared vision-language sphere → a committed stage through the validation gate). On top of it Stage-JEPA predicts the next K stages: which phase comes next, when it starts (ETA) and what it will look like — as a latent, decoded by retrieval (nearest real window, shown as footage) and by a small latent→pixel decoder (a mock-up, not a JEPA output). The learned heads are zero-initialised residuals on the counting grammar: at initialisation the twin is the grammar. This recording is one execution with 9 stages, so the evidence below is within-recording and honest about what the learned part adds (mostly: nothing yet — see card 1).
