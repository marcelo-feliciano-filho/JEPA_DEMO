The encoder was trained self-supervised on this exact video. The phases below were found by clustering its embeddings; the phase names were assigned by a human afterwards (phase_names.json).

Actual footage. The colour = the phase the model assigned to this moment.

Each window of the video → a point. The clusters are the phases the model discovered on its own.
3 · Phase-boundary signal (no labels)
Cosine distance between consecutive windows. Spikes mark where the embedding crosses between clusters — i.e. a phase transition.
