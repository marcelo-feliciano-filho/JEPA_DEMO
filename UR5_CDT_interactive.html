Cognitive Digital Twin — what is shown, what is not

Perception is VL-JEPA (every window → a language-grounded phase). Cognition is two experts on top of it: a sparse MoE latent world model (GRU context → noisy top-k router → experts predict the latent at +0.17…+1.0 s, with a novelty gate) and a symbolic grammar of the task learned from the phase stream (transition graph + dwell times). Cards 1–4 are measured within this single recording (blocked temporal CV or perturbed video re-encoded through VL-JEPA): they show expectations and their violations, not knowledge of the process. Card 5 is the test that would show process knowledge — next actions on executions the twin has never seen — and it cannot be run on one recording; it is shown on a simulated process and labelled as such.

1 · Announces the next phase (this recording)

5/8out-of-fold next-phase predictions correct, announced 1.0 s before the switch (chance 25%, majority-next 38%, binomial p = 0.027). supported
By a hand-designed counting model of transitions and dwell times, fitted on the other blocks of the same recording. Latent expert alone: lead 0.06 s.

2 · Notices violated expectations

3/4perturbed videos detected (freeze · reverse · speed ×3 · jump cut), false alarms on the normal video: 4 windows (0.6%). A change detector scores AUROC 0.15 on the freeze — it sees nothing happening and is content; the twin scores 0.54 because a 4 s pause violates its dwell-time expectation. supported

3 · Discovers regimes

NMI 0.31between out-of-fold expert choice and phase; chance 0.17 (circular-shift permutation, p = 0.022). supported

4 · Uses time (ablations)

50%anticipation accuracy at +1 s with the full model (persistence 54%); 54% with the context order shuffled, 54% with no context. not supported

6 · Many lines, many robots (simulated)

6×more alarms when a line's twin watches another line's robots than its own (0.4 vs 2.7 per 100 steps). Fleet of 240 concurrent streams, 32 perturbed: precision 55%, recall 94%, F1 0.69. synthetic multi-line study

5 · Knows the process? (unseen executions)

not testablefor the UR5 recording — it is one execution, so generalisation to new executions cannot be tested and no claim is made.
Simulated process (24 executions, leave-executions-out, learned GRU predictor, nothing hand-written): next action on unseen executions 80% (learned GRU; deep MoE with expert-embedding routing 84% named, 0.33 ms/step) with the new perception (attentive pooling + time-contrastive; 38% with the original mean-pooled VL-JEPA) vs chance 17%, time-shuffled control 14%; oracle perception 86% (Bayes-optimal ≈ 86 %). protocol shown on synthetic data only
